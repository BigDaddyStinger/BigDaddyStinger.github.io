Unity · C# · Dash-runner

Knight Dash

Forced forward movement, obstacle dodging, stamina management, and bullet-hell elements.

Development Process

I built a tight dash loop and stamina system to push risk and reward while the knight automatically advances. Level lanes and rock obstacles create readable micro-puzzles. Stamina prevents repeated dash spamming, making timing matter.

Playtesting focused on hitbox fairness and dash responsiveness under constant motion.

The Challenge

Balancing stamina drain against the feel of dashing during forced movement.

The Solution

I tuned dash invulnerability frames and recovery, adjusted obstacle spacing, and added cooldown buffers for consistency.

What I Learned

Clear telegraphing and generous input buffers do more for flow in an auto-scroller than raw difficulty.

Unity · C# · Boss AI

Box Man Boss Fight

A C# state machine with three escalating phases, readable telegraphs, and pattern mixing.

Prefer a separate player? Watch the gameplay on YouTube ↗

Development Process

I implemented a phase-based boss using a script-based state machine and health thresholds to trigger behavior changes. Animation Events gate hit windows, while cooldowns and anticipation frames keep the attacks patterned.

Three Escalating Phases

  • Phase 1: Staple Throw, Kick, and Normal Swing introduce simple, punishable moves.
  • Phase 2: Swing Combo 1, Swing Combo 2, 360 Swing, and a hailstorm multi-staple barrage increase pressure.
  • Phase 3 — Enraged: Adds Slam, increases global boss speed to 1.25×, and tightens timings and recovery windows.

The Challenge

Unintended transition loops and mistimed hitboxes during combos.

The Solution

Explicit exit conditions per state, normalized transition durations, rooted windups, and event-driven hitbox toggles.

What I Learned

Telegraphs and recovery windows matter more than raw damage. Even a small speed increase, such as 1.25×, dramatically changes the pressure of an encounter.
